# B6 — Render: villager poses — carry, stir, eat, shiver, hearts

Fits: batch 2's jobs need bodies that show them. Read `DESIGN.md` §2 (gentle motion) and §3.2.

## Deliverable (`src/render/villagers.ts`, palette if needed)

1. **Carry pose** — while `villager.carrying`: a small log (cylinder, `PALETTE.trunk`) appears held
   forward at chest height; arms raise; slight forward lean. Vanishes on deposit. Eased in/out.
2. **Stir pose** — while `state === 'working' && task === 'cook'`: lean toward the pot and a small
   circular stir with one arm (~1.2 Hz, gentle).
3. **Eat garnish** — on an `eat` event for this villager (`state.events`): two or three tiny hearts
   (canvas-drawn `CanvasTexture` sprites, `PALETTE.flowerPink`-toned) rise ~1 u and fade over ~1.2 s;
   also a subtle "savoring" head bob while the rest lasts. No hearts for plain rests.
4. **Shiver** — when `fire.fuel === 0` and the villager is `idle` or `resting`: a faint, fast tremble
   (≤ ±0.006 u, ~7 Hz) and a slight hunch. Never distracting.
5. All additions ease in/out; nothing snaps; no new draw calls per villager beyond the carry log and
   transient hearts (hearts are pooled/reused, max ~4).
